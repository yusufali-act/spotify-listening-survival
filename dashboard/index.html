What the data says

Generated automatically from the numbers each time the pipeline runs.

Listening over time, and its eras

Each month is described by the share of time spent on each artist. Neighbouring months that sound alike are merged by hierarchical clustering until the clearest set of eras remains (picked by silhouette score).

Survival analysis

The same methods actuaries use for mortality and lapse, applied to music. Plays and artists that are still going at the end of the data are censored: they count while observed, without pretending to know when they'd end.

How long does an artist stay in rotation?

Kaplan–Meier curve. An artist “dies” after 90 days without a play. Shaded bands are 95% Greenwood intervals.

The skip mortality table

q(x): of the songs still playing at second x, the share skipped in the next 5 seconds.

Most played vs most loved

Raw play counts reward habits and autoplay. The love score weights each play by how much was heard and whether it was chosen or replayed on purpose, then applies Bühlmann credibility (k = 20 plays) so a track needs a track record before it can rank highly.
